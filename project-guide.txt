*PAYPAL AI HACKATHON: PART 1, THE PROJECT*

*The hackathon*
- Deadline: 12 Nov, 12pm PST = 13 Nov, 1am PKT. We submit on 11 Nov.
- Prizes: $67,500 cash. Judged equally on technical implementation, design, impact, innovation and presentation.
- We must submit: a working demo, a public GitHub repo with a license, a public YouTube demo video under 3 minutes, and a text description.
- PayPal sandbox and AI must both be central to the project.

*The problem*
Small sellers run their business through Instagram and WhatsApp. Taking orders and payments by chat is messy, and building a store is too much work.

*The solution*
A seller uploads product photos. AI turns them into a store listing, and the app publishes a shareable storefront with PayPal checkout. An AI assistant helps customers buy, and helps the seller manage orders.

*Core features (must work before any extra)*
1. Photo to listing: seller uploads 3-5 photos. A vision LLM drafts title, description, category, variants and tags. The seller edits and sets the price. AI never invents prices.
2. Storefront: public page /shop/seller-name showing the listings.
3. PayPal checkout: Orders API in sandbox, single-item purchase.
4. Webhooks: paid orders are recorded and stock updates automatically.
5. Customer assistant: answers product questions using only the listing data. If it does not know, it says so.
6. Seller dashboard: orders list, AI-written order summary, restock suggestions based on actual sales.

*Extra features (only after the core works)*
7. Multilingual storefront and assistant (English, Urdu, Arabic, etc.). The original listing stays the source of truth; translations are generated from it.
8. Chat-to-checkout cart: the assistant adds items to a cart from the conversation, then hands off to PayPal for the cart total. Prices and stock come from our database. The total is computed by code, never by the model. A confirm-cart step comes before payment.
9. AI-assisted refunds with seller approval (stretch): the assistant checks the refund policy and drafts a recommendation. The seller approves or rejects, and approval triggers the PayPal refund. If it slips, we cut it and list it under next steps.

*Stack*
React frontend, Node/Express backend, simple DB (Postgres or SQLite), a vision-capable LLM API, PayPal sandbox, free host (webhooks need a public URL).

*Team rules*
- The core must work smoothly before any extra feature starts.
- AI never decides prices, totals or stock. Those come from the database and code.
- The assistant answers only from our data. No made-up product details.
- A human approval step comes before any money action (cart confirm, refund).
- No fake metrics. Restock suggestions are rules plus LLM wording, not a predictive model, and we say that plainly. Demo data is labeled as demo data.
- If a feature is half-working on submission day, hide it instead of demoing it.
- Merge small and often. Keep the main branch deployable.
- Secrets (API keys, PayPal credentials) live only in .env and are never committed.

*Demo story (2-3 min)*
1. Seller uploads photos, AI generates the listing, seller edits and publishes.
2. A customer opens the storefront, asks a question in Urdu, adds items through chat, and pays through PayPal sandbox.
3. The seller dashboard updates instantly with the order, stock change and AI summary.
4. (If done) A refund request is recommended by AI and approved by the seller.

*Known risks*
- PayPal sandbox quirks: test early, do not leave integration for last.
- Webhooks need a public URL: deploy early.
- Crowded idea: the assistant and restock advice must feel central, so they get real time.
- Only if cart (extra 8) slips: we change the demo to a single-item checkout. We do not fake it.
